# TA-03 · Before you trust the screenshot

Published: 2026-10-11  
Subject: Evidence and context

A screenshot may show real words while omitting the address, date or surrounding paragraph needed to interpret them.

## Editorial scope

For each contested claim, identify the claim, source, observation and known limits. State when the original source could not be recovered.

## Reading-edition summary

A cropped image is one observation, not the complete record. Preserve the source context wherever possible and do not hide uncertainty behind confident wording.
