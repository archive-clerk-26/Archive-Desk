# TA-02 · The name on the page

Published: 2026-10-07  
Subject: Digital identity

Names and usernames are clues about public accounts. They are not sufficient evidence that two accounts belong to the same person.

## Editorial scope

Prefer direct links between public sources to a guessed identity match. Treat publication credit, authorship and account ownership as different claims.

## Reading-edition summary

A project can have editors, maintainers and contributors. A visible credit may explain responsibility for a particular page without establishing who wrote every part of it.
